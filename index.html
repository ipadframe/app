Support

How Can We Help?

How do I connect my photos?

Frame connects directly to your iCloud Photo Library. Simply grant permission when prompted, and you'll be able to select any album or collection to display.

Does Frame work in landscape and portrait?

Yes! Frame automatically adapts to however you position your iPad. Your photos will be beautifully displayed in any orientation.

Will Frame drain my battery?

Frame is optimized for extended display. We recommend keeping your iPad plugged in for the best experience.
