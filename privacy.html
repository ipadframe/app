Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Frame does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.

Your Privacy

Frame is designed with your privacy in mind. The app operates entirely on your device and does not transmit any information to external servers.

Photos

Frame accesses your photo library only to display your images on screen. Your photos never leave your device and are not uploaded, analyzed, or shared in any way.

Analytics

Frame does not include any analytics, tracking, or telemetry. We have no way of knowing how you use the app.

Third Parties

Frame does not integrate with any third-party services that collect data. There are no ads, no social features, and no external dependencies that track you.
